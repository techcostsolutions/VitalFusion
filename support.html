Frequently asked questions

A few good places to start.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open VitalFusion and follow the in-app prompt. You will be able to choose the Health categories you want to share. You can change or revoke these permissions later in the Health app or iOS Settings.

Which devices work with VitalFusion?

VitalFusion is designed for iPhone, Apple Watch, Apple Health, and compatible Bluetooth smart rings. Exact compatibility and minimum software versions should be listed here when the app is released.

How do I disconnect a smart ring?

Use the device connection controls in VitalFusion. You can also manage Bluetooth permissions in iOS Settings. Disconnecting stops future access; information already saved on your device remains until you remove it.

Are the insights medical advice?

No. VitalFusion is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Its metrics and insights are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Discuss health concerns with a qualified professional.

How do I share a report?

When report sharing is available, you choose exactly when to create and send a report using the iOS share sheet. Please review the recipient and the information included before sending.
