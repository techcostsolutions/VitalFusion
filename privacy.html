Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

1. Who we are

VitalFusion (“VitalFusion,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a wellness application that helps you view information from Apple Health, Apple Watch, compatible Bluetooth smart rings, and other sources you choose to connect. For privacy questions, contact us at info@techcostsolutions.com.

2. Health and wearable information

With your explicit permission, VitalFusion may read health and fitness data made available through Apple Health/HealthKit, Apple Watch, and supported Bluetooth smart rings. Depending on the permissions you grant and devices you connect, this may include activity, steps, calories, workouts, sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood oxygen, wrist temperature, and similar wellness metrics.

We access only the categories you authorize. You can change Health permissions in the Health app and device permissions in iOS Settings at any time. Bluetooth ring data is accessed only after you choose to connect a supported device.

3. How we use information

We use your information to provide the features you request: displaying health metrics, identifying trends, creating wellness insights, calculating optional readiness-style scores, and producing reports that you choose to view or share. AI-assisted insights, where available, are designed to summarize or contextualize your data; they are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
