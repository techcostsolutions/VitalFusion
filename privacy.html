Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Before publishing: replace the bracketed contact details below with your live support email and legal business name. This policy must always match the app’s actual data practices and App Store privacy disclosures.

1. Who we are

VitalFusion (“VitalFusion,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a wellness application that helps you view information from Apple Health, Apple Watch, compatible Bluetooth smart rings, and other sources you choose to connect. For privacy questions, contact us at info@techcostsolutions.com.

2. Health and wearable information

With your explicit permission, VitalFusion may read health and fitness data made available through Apple Health/HealthKit, Apple Watch, and supported Bluetooth smart rings. Depending on the permissions you grant and devices you connect, this may include activity, steps, calories, workouts, sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood oxygen, wrist temperature, and similar wellness metrics.

We access only the categories you authorize. You can change Health permissions in the Health app and device permissions in iOS Settings at any time. Bluetooth ring data is accessed only after you choose to connect a supported device.

3. How we use information

We use your information to provide the features you request: displaying health metrics, identifying trends, creating wellness insights, calculating optional readiness-style scores, and producing reports that you choose to view or share. AI-assisted insights, where available, are designed to summarize or contextualize your data; they are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

We do not use HealthKit data or other health information for advertising, behavioral advertising, marketing profiles, data brokerage, or sale to third parties. We do not disclose health information to advertisers.

4. Where information is stored

VitalFusion is designed to process and store health information on your device by default. We do not operate an account-based cloud sync service at launch.

If optional cloud synchronization, backup, or account features are introduced later, we will clearly explain what data is involved, obtain any required consent, update this policy before the change takes effect, and provide controls for opting in or out. Any optional cloud feature will not change the permissions you have already granted to Apple Health or your connected devices.

5. Reports and sharing

VitalFusion may let you create a report or share selected information using iOS sharing tools. Sharing is initiated by you. Once you send information to another person or service, their privacy practices apply. Please review the recipient and content before sharing.

6. Limited technical information

To operate and improve the app, we may process limited non-health technical information such as app version, device and operating-system version, language, crash diagnostics, and feature-performance information. We use this information to maintain security, diagnose problems, and improve reliability. We do not combine it with HealthKit data for advertising or sell it.

7. Service providers and disclosures

We do not sell personal information. We may use carefully selected service providers to provide essential functions such as crash reporting, customer support, or—if you opt in—future cloud services. Providers may process information only on our instructions and only for the services they provide. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect safety and rights, or in connection with a business transaction, subject to applicable law.

8. Retention and deletion

Information stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app or remove the information through the relevant device controls, subject to iOS behavior and backups you manage. If we introduce an optional account or cloud service, we will publish clear retention and deletion instructions. You may contact us at the address above with privacy requests.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please keep your device protected with a passcode and current software.

10. Children

VitalFusion is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child provided us personal information, contact us so we can address the request.

11. International users

If you use VitalFusion outside the country where our services or providers operate, your information may be processed in other countries with different privacy laws. Where applicable, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to VitalFusion or applicable law. We will post the updated policy here and revise the effective date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice where required.
